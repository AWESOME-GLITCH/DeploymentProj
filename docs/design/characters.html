Things they hold

Blobert, the blob who clears mistakes

He is the only thing that ever handles an error, so a mistake is never red, never a loss, always a small laugh. One gag per miss, never the same twice in a row. Pass him the screen's one block colour; on a block-colour card pass cream instead.

Poses

in, look, wave, out · plus gag, below

The six gags, 880 ms each

Every card plays the whole scripted miss: pop in, look at the wrong word, the gag, a wave, slide out. Press a button to play one, or play them all in turn.

Every gag, frame by frame

Rendered from the same timeline with the progress prop, so what the videos draw and what the browser animates cannot drift apart.

His timing

  • in 0 to 200 ms
  • look 200 to 320 ms
  • gag 320 to 680 ms
  • wave 560 to 720 ms
  • out 720 to 880 ms
  • The wave overlaps the tail of the gag by 120 ms, which is what brings the whole miss under 900 ms.

Using it

  • <Maya pose="talk" size={240} /> on the coach screens.
  • <Leo pose="hold" item="cup" /> in the cafe lesson.
  • <Character name="you" pose="celebrate" stickers idle /> on the reward card.
  • <Sticker kind="star" size={28} twinkle /> for the good-answer pop.
  • One block colour per screen. Keep seed fixed so videos stay frame stable.
